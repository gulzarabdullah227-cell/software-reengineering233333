📖 About AstroBot

AstroBot is a friendly chatbot that answers simple questions about space. Its answers come from a CSV file, so they are short and easy to read.

How to use

  1. Press Start Chat.
  2. Type a question, or tap a suggested question.
  3. Read the short answer and ask the next one!

Topics

☀️ Solar System🌙 Moon⭐ Stars🛰️ Missions✨ Fun Facts

Tips

  • Keep questions short, like “Why is Mars red?”
  • Small spelling mistakes are okay.
  • If I don’t know, try a suggested question.
